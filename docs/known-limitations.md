# Geographic limitations — CartoRig 1.2.3

- Admin 1 / city coverage is **complete relative to the two pinned Natural Earth 1:10m sources**, but does not extend beyond those sources and does not promise street addresses or every small municipality or locality worldwide.
- Country basemap is Natural Earth 110m; the detailed polygons of Admin 1 and cities are Natural Earth 10m. Country map may therefore look visually simplified at extremely high zoom and is not intended for legal/cadastral use.
- Original source includes seven unnamed administrative features. Their true geometries and IDs are preserved without inventing human-readable names.
- Some country/area names have regional or political disputes; the editorial positions in the pinned Natural Earth source are not modified by CartoRig.
- Portuguese/local-language search and display use supplied source multilingual names and curated aliases; arbitrary place-name translations are not guaranteed for every feature.
- The browser-based scene composer is **not** a DaVinci Resolve native plugin nor a GIS survey tool.
- Full datasets are verified as embedded, and the Windows executables were cross-compiled, but native Windows setup/launch has **not** been independently run in this environment. Execute the automated native acceptance pipeline before claiming certification.
